one for every need

WhiteEqual power at all frequencies — like a fan or TV static. Masks distractions, shortens sleep onset.
PinkSofter than white noise — like steady rain or ocean waves. Boosts deep sleep and memory.
BrownDeep bass rumble like thunder or a waterfall. Calms anxiety, deepens sleep and focus.
VioletHigh-frequency sharp hiss. Used for tinnitus relief and intense cognitive focus.
GrayPerceptually flat — equally loud at all frequencies. The easiest on the ears for long sessions.
BlueBrighter than white noise — a light airy hiss. Keeps you alert during monotonous tasks.
